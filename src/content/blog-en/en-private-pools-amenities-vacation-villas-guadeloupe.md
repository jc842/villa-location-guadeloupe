# Private Pools & Essential Amenities for a Dream Guadeloupe Vacation

Selecting a vacation villa requires attention to specific island features that guarantee an effortless Caribbean escape.

## Essential Villa Features

1. **Independent Water Cistern**: Ensures uninterrupted freshwater supply during regional utility network maintenance.
2. **Open-Air Living Veranda (*Varangue*)**: The quintessential architectural feature where indoor living flows seamlessly onto outdoor dining decks.
3. **Private Swimming Pool with Child Safety**: Mandatory alarm or barrier protection adhering strictly to French safety standards.
