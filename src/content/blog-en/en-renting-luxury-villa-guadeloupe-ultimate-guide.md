# Renting a Luxury Villa in Guadeloupe: Pools, Beaches & Island Concierge

Guadeloupe offers a diverse island landscape where butterfly-shaped wings present two contrasting vacation experiences: the coral beaches of Grande-Terre and the lush tropical rainforest of Basse-Terre.

## Prime Villa Locations

- **Saint-François & Pointe des Châteaux**: The premier lagoon playground featuring luxury modern villas with private infinity pools, nearby golf, and marina dining.
- **Sainte-Anne**: Renowned for turquoise protected lagoons (Plage de la Caravelle, Bois Jolan) and oceanfront private estates.
- **Deshaies (Basse-Terre)**: Caribbean hillside villas nestled between tropical canopy and golden sands (*Death in Paradise* filming enclave).

Private villa rentals deliver unmatched freedom, privacy, and authentic Creole living.
